Straight talk on scraping

What works: county appraisal districts and parcel GIS portals. They're public record, most publish an open ArcGIS/REST feed, and nobody blocks you. That's your assessed value, sqft, year built, tax, and ownership.
What doesn't: scraping Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com or the MLS directly. They ban the IP within minutes, the HTML shifts weekly, and it breaks their terms of use. Any tool that claims otherwise breaks within a month. Use a licensed data API instead — that's what the API tab is for.
The practical stack: county scraper for the assessment side (free) + a listings/AVM API for the market side (~free to $50/mo) + your own eyes on the photos. That combination is legal, stable, and covers everything this calculator needs.
